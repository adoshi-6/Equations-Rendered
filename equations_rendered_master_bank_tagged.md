**Legend**
- ✅ Buildable now — fits the current 2D PIL-canvas rendering engine (curves, parametric/polar plots, fractals, pixel-grid scalar fields, vector fields, particle systems, graphs, etc.)
- ❌ Needs a real 3D (or 4D) rendering subsystem — the engine currently has **no 3D rendering at all** (no camera, no mesh/wireframe, no perspective projection — confirmed by reading `renderer.py`, `drawing_utils.py`, and every simulation module). **Not deleted** — keep these in the queue for after the 3D engine is added.

Where an item's traditional visualization is ambiguous (could be shown as a 2D analogy/projection *or* a true 3D object), I tagged ✅ and noted the 2D approach the engine would actually use — flag me if you want the "real" 3D version instead once the 3D engine exists.
